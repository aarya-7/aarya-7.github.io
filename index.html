Projects

End-to-end analytics projects focused on revenue intelligence, customer health, and conversion optimization — built with PostgreSQL, Python, and Tableau.

E-commerce Funnel Analysis

Identified revenue leakage across 1.7M user sessions by modeling a View → Cart → Transaction funnel in PostgreSQL — quantifying drop-off at each stage and recommending 3 A/B tests (cart recovery, PDP clarity, category UX) with primary and guardrail metrics. Delivered 4 Tableau dashboards covering funnel health, segmentation, and time-to-convert.

GMV Anomaly Detection & Attribution

Built a real-time revenue monitoring system that flags abnormal GMV swings before they compound — detecting 17 anomalies across 409 days with severity tiers and category-level root cause attribution. Delivered an executive drill-down dashboard enabling faster, more targeted revenue investigations.

SaaS Retention & Churn Analytics

Designed a proactive churn prevention system across 500 accounts — surfacing at-risk customers before cancellation using cohort retention metrics (M1: 90.1%, M3: 76.5%, M6: 57.4%) and an explainable early warning model with risk bands and churn reason codes to directly support retention strategy.

Tableau Dashboards

Interactive dashboards built on Tableau Public — covering funnel health, anomaly detection, and SaaS retention.

About Me

I'm Aarya Bhivsanee, a Master's student in Data Science at Stevens Institute of Technology (GPA: 3.55), graduating May 2026. I hold a Bachelor's in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from Savitribai Phule Pune University (GPA: 8.3/10). I specialize in building analytics systems — from raw data pipelines to executive dashboards — that help teams make faster, more grounded decisions.

My project work spans e-commerce conversion optimization, real-time revenue anomaly detection, and SaaS churn prevention. I also serve as a Graduate Peer Leader at Stevens Institute of Technology, mentoring incoming graduate students through academic and professional transitions.

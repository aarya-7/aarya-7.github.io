RECENT PROJECTS

E-commerce Funnel Analysis

Analyzed 1.7 million user sessions to locate where revenue was leaking across the checkout funnel. Quantified drop-off at each stage from View to Cart to Transaction, then proposed 3 A/B tests targeting cart recovery, product page clarity, and category UX with defined primary and guardrail metrics. Delivered 4 Tableau dashboards covering funnel health, segmentation, and time-to-convert.

GMV Anomaly Detection & Attribution

Built a revenue monitoring system that automatically flags abnormal GMV movements before they escalate. Detected 17 anomalies across 409 days and classified each by severity and spike or drop direction. An interactive Tableau drill-down dashboard attributes each anomaly to specific product categories, giving revenue teams the context to act quickly.

SaaS Retention & Churn Analytics

Designed a churn prevention system across 500 accounts that identifies at-risk customers before they cancel. Cohort retention tracked at M1 (90.1%), M3 (76.5%), and M6 (57.4%) showed where drop-off concentrated. A behavioral early warning model produces risk bands and churn reason codes, giving customer success teams a prioritized list for proactive outreach.

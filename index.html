ABOUT ME

I work in data analytics, and the thread running through everything I do is customer behavior — specifically why people stop doing the thing they were doing.

Churn, cart abandonment, a sudden revenue drop — it’s all the same instinct to me: finding the exact moment things broke, and why. Sometimes that moment is earlier than expected.

E-commerce Funnel

Everyone assumed the biggest drop-off was at checkout. It wasn’t. The real collapse happened much earlier — at the point where browsing was supposed to turn into intent.

SaaS Churn

The pattern that stuck with me wasn’t the churn rate itself — it was that the customers we lost were worth more on average than the ones we kept. Concentrated in mid-sized accounts: big enough to matter, small enough that nobody was personally watching them. That reframed churn as an attention problem, not just a product problem.

Revenue Anomalies

Flagging the weird days was never the hard part. Explaining them was. So the system didn’t just say revenue dropped — it said which category caused it.

I’m exploring data analyst and product analyst roles — anywhere that wants someone who treats numbers as a way to understand people, not just report on them.

RECENT PROJECTS

E-commerce Funnel Analysis

  • Pinpointed the primary revenue leak at View→Cart (2.21% conversion, 97.79% drop) rather than checkout — shifting optimization priority to the product page and add-to-cart experience across 1.76M sessions
  • Found returning visitors converted at 3× the rate of new visitors during peak hours, informing 3 A/B tests (PDP clarity, category UX, cart recovery) with defined primary and guardrail metrics
  • Built a session-level clickstream mart in PostgreSQL (sessionization, window functions) and delivered 4 Tableau dashboards covering funnel health, segmentation, and time-to-convert

GMV Anomaly Detection & Attribution

  • Reduced time-to-root-cause from hours to zero by engineering a pipeline that detects, decomposes, and narrates GMV anomalies automatically — surfacing 12 flagged revenue events across 18 months with severity tagging and category attribution
  • Eliminated analyst write-up time by constraining an LLM to pre-computed fact packets only, producing auditable What/Why/Action briefs — a +558% Black Friday spike (z=29.79) attributed to Southeast Brazil at 72% of total delta with no manual query
  • Improved detection robustness over standard deviation using MAD-based robust z-scores on detrended residuals, validated against 3 synthetic stress-test shocks

SaaS Retention & Churn Analytics

  • Quantified $9.9M total paid MRR against an 8.02% weighted churn rate, isolating the 21–50 seat segment ($165K churned MRR, highest of any range) as the concentration point — reframing churn as an attention problem in mid-market accounts
  • Validated risk-band scoring against actual churn across 500 accounts: HIGH-risk churned at 50% vs. 15% (MEDIUM) and 7% (LOW), driven primarily by usage drop and elevated error rate
  • Identified the first 90 days as the primary churn lever via cohort retention (M1: 90.1%, M3: 76.5%, M6: 57.4%), pointing retention strategy toward onboarding fixes over long-term engagement programs
